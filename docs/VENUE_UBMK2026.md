**Format / rules (verify).**

- Languages: English **or** Turkish (same proceedings). We submit in English.
- Review: ≥2 referees (avg 2.3 in 2025); third on disagreement.
- **In-person presentation is MANDATORY — no remote/online option.** At least one author must attend.
- Fees (2026, verify): ~450 USD individual / 250 USD student / 350 USD member, +20% VAT.
- Submission via UBMK's own portal (not EasyChair); historically also email .docx+.pdf to ubmkkonferansi@gmail.com — **verify whether a LaTeX PDF alone is accepted or a .docx is required.**

**AI-use declaration (REQUIRED — plan for both forms).**

2. As an IEEE-published venue it **also** inherits the IEEE rule: disclose AI-generated content in the
   **Acknowledgments**, name the AI system, identify the sections and level of use. AI cannot be an author.
